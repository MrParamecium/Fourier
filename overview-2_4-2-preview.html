Graphical Understanding of Convolution Operation

Convolution becomes easier to reason about when you draw the two signals, flip one, and slide it across the other. This section shows how overlap and signed area build one output value at a time.

After this overview

  • Identify the interval where two signals overlap.
  • Track how shifting changes the product.
  • Use the signed area to explain the output.
